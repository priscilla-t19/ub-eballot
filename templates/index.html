How to Vote
Four simple steps to cast your ballot
1
Find the Election

Scroll down to Active Elections and click Vote Now on the election you want to participate in.

2
Enter Your Student ID

Type in your UB student number. You must be a registered UB student to be eligible to vote.

3
Verify via Email

A 6-digit code is sent to your registered email. Enter it to confirm it's really you voting.

4
Cast & Get Receipt

Select your candidates and submit. You'll receive a receipt code to verify your vote was counted.
